From zero to deal channel

The affiliate deal-channel playbook

You don't hold inventory and you don't buy anything. You surface price gaps that already exist, attach a referral link, and get paid a commission when a follower buys. Here's the whole machine, in build order.

Why it works

You never touch product or money. The retailer fulfills, ships, and handles returns. You are paid for the introduction — win or lose on that specific sale, the deal itself is real.

02Sourcing
Where the deals actually come from

Mine price arbitrage, don't hunt sales

The margin on those D&G flip-flops ($235 vs $375 retail) isn't a flash sale — it's a European boutique selling to the US with duties included. Same product, structurally cheaper source. That's the well you draw from every day.

  • ModeSens price history — search any item, it shows the cheapest global seller and whether the price just dropped.
  • Cross-border boutiques — Cettire, Italist, LN-CC, Gente Roma. Euro pricing + duties-included often beats US retail by 25–40%.
  • Outlet/flash rails — YOOX, TheOutnet, Nordstrom Rack, The RealReal for end-of-season and pre-owned.
  • Set price alerts — ModeSens and Google Shopping alerts on the 20–30 "hype" pieces your audience actually wants.
The filter

Only post items with a clean retail anchor — a recognizable brand where "$X vs $Y retail" is obvious at a glance. Margin without a familiar name doesn't convert.

03Package
Turn a price gap into a post

Cloak the link, write the anchor

Two things happen to every deal before it ships: the affiliate tag gets hidden, and the price is framed against retail. The Console tab does both — the caption generator produces the exact "cost vs retail + size + link" format, and your affiliate tag is appended automatically.

  • Shorten & cloak — run the tagged link through Bitly or Dub.co. Hides the referral tag and gives you click tracking (those 664/753 view counts).
  • Anchor the price — always "$616 vs $790 retail." The contrast is the product.
  • Show it on-body — pair the product cutout with a styled/worn shot, exactly like the channel's split images.
04Distribute
Firehose + funnel

Telegram broadcast, Instagram top of funnel

The channel runs 1,749 Telegram subscribers as the paid-attention firehose, with Instagram recruiting them. Split the jobs deliberately.

  • Telegram broadcast channel — where the deal volume goes. Frictionless links, no algorithm suppressing outbound URLs.
  • Instagram / TikTok — reels and stories that showcase 1–2 hero deals and push "full drops in the Telegram — link in bio."
  • Cadence — 3–8 curated deals a day beats 40 mediocre ones. Every post needs real margin or the list goes numb.
